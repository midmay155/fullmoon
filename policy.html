01Summary

Rằm has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you write stays on your device, encrypted, and it leaves the device only if you choose to back it up, in a file that is encrypted with your own password.

  • No sign-up, no login, and we never collect your name or email address.
  • Your diary entries, photos and saved locations are never uploaded automatically. The only way they leave your device is a backup that you start, encrypted with a password only you know.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone.
  • Diary content and photos are encrypted before being written to storage.
  • The free version shows banner ads (provided by Google AdMob). Rằm Pro removes them and is bought through Google Play, so we never see your payment details.

Besides backups you start yourself, the only data that leaves your device is crash reports, anonymous usage statistics, advertising data and purchase status. Section 05 covers each.

02What we do not collect

Rằm neither requests nor collects:

  • Your name, email, phone number, date of birth, or any identifying details.
  • Your contacts, messages, call history, or system calendar.
  • What you write in your diary — we have no way to read it, and no way to read your backups.
  • Photos from your gallery, other than the ones you add to a diary entry yourself.

Because there are no accounts and no server of ours storing content, we cannot view, back up or restore your diary — even if you ask us to. Backups are created and restored by you, and they are encrypted so that only your password can open them.

Encryption

Diary entries and photos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is generated randomly on your own device and protected by the Android Keystore, an OS-managed key store from which keys cannot be exported.

This means that if someone extracts the data files from your device, they cannot decrypt them. It does not protect against someone holding your unlocked device — that is what the PIN and your screen lock are for.

Diary lock

You can set a 6-digit PIN, with biometrics if your device supports them, to lock the diary section specifically. While locked, diary content is also hidden from home screen widgets. Five wrong attempts trigger a 30-second lockout, which grows with further failures.

If you forget the PIN, use the recovery code issued when you enabled the lock. If you lose both the PIN and the recovery code, no one can unlock it, including us. The only remaining option is clearing the app's data, which erases the diary.

Automatic backup is disabled

The app disables Android's automatic backup, because the encryption key is tied to the device and is not included in system backups — a restored copy could not be decrypted. As a consequence, your diary does not carry over automatically when you switch or reset devices. Use the app's own backup (next section) to move your diary to another device.

Backup and restore (started by you)

In Settings you can export your diary entries and photos to a backup file, and import them again later. Locations saved with entries are included. A backup is created only when you ask for it.

  • Encrypted with your password. You choose a password of at least 8 characters. The backup is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from that password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). The password is never stored and never sent anywhere. If you forget it, nobody can open the backup, including us.
  • Save to a file. You choose where the file is saved. After that, the file is under your control; keep it somewhere safe.
  • Save to Google Drive (optional). If you choose this, the app asks Google for permission to use its own hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). With that permission the app can see only the backup file it created. It cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files. The file is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded, so Google stores only an encrypted file. We never receive the file, your password or your Google account details, and the upload goes directly from your device to Google. It uses your own Drive storage space. Only the most recent Drive backup is kept; creating a new one replaces the previous one.
  • Restoring. Importing a backup adds only the days that are missing on your device. A day that already has an entry is left unchanged.

To remove the Drive backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Rằm and choose Delete hidden app data. To withdraw the app's access, open your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services, and remove Rằm.

Coordinates are rounded to three decimal places, roughly 100-metre precision, and stored encrypted alongside the entry. Declining location permission affects no other feature.

05Third-party services

These are all the places where data may leave your device:

Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes, a report is sent to Firebase so we can find and fix the fault. Reports contain the error type and its location in our source code, device model and Android version, app version, and a random installation identifier. Reports never contain your diary content, photos or location.

Firebase Analytics

Collects aggregate usage statistics — which screens are opened, which features are used, how many sessions are crash-free — so we know what to improve. This data is not linked to your identity.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version shows banner advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Rằm Pro removes all ads, and no ad is shown or loaded for Pro members. Diary content, photos and locations are never shared with advertisers.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad data is collected, and you can change your choice in the app's settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

Rằm Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google, and we never receive or store your card or payment details. The app receives only the purchase status needed to unlock Pro, and keeps a record of your active plan on the device. Subscriptions are managed and can be cancelled at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Purchases are governed by the Google Play Terms of Service.

Google Drive (optional backup)

Used only if you choose to back up to Google Drive; see the section on backup above for what is stored, how it is protected and how to remove it. Google handles your sign-in and consent.

How long this data is kept

Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically. We keep no copy of either outside Firebase.

Transmission security

All of these services transmit over HTTPS with TLS encryption. They are provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

06Children

Rằm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with data, please get in touch — although, since the app has no accounts and stores nothing on a server, we hold no data to delete.

07Your rights

  • View and edit your data — all of it lives in the app, where you can read and change it directly.
  • Delete your data — delete individual entries in the app, or everything at once by uninstalling, or via Settings → Apps → Rằm → Storage → Clear data.
  • Withdraw permissions — revoke location or notification access at any time in system settings.
  • Stop sending diagnostics — uninstalling the app ends all crash reporting and analytics immediately; nothing further is sent.
  • Delete your backups — delete backup files you saved yourself. For Google Drive, use Delete hidden app data as described above.
  • Manage ads and purchases — change ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and cancel a subscription in Google Play.

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California, you have additional rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability under local law. Because we store none of your data on any server, these rights are exercised directly on your device.
